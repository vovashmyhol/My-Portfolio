About Me

Hi, my name is Volodymyr.

I started developing simply out of curiosity, because I have always enjoyed learning something new. At one point, I had an old laptop that cost around $100, and I was interested in understanding how bots and various services inside Telegram worked.

It all started with the creation of the VOCO channel, where I shared my projects and ideas with just a few dozen readers. Over time, the channel grew, and it now has hundreds of readers.

At first, these were small experiments, but eventually, my first full-fledged projects appeared. I started working with Telegram bots, Mini Apps, the Telegram API, and web interfaces. Gradually, development became more than just a way to create something — it became a genuine passion.

I enjoy coming up with my own ideas and bringing them to life from scratch. Sometimes it is a small bot, sometimes a Mini App, and sometimes a project that I can redesign dozens of times until it looks exactly the way I envisioned it.

I have never aimed simply to release as many projects as possible. I am more interested in trying new things, learning from my mistakes, and creating products that I would genuinely want to use myself. I try to bring every Mini App as close to perfection as possible, because design and performance are my top priorities.

After some time, I decided that I was ready to work for other people and get paid for it. But after completing my first order, I started wondering: why not try working for myself?

It was a bold and risky decision. My projects could have failed, and in that case, I would have lost my time, energy, and money spent on servers. Nevertheless, I decided to give it a try and became an independent developer, gaining the freedom to choose what to create and how to create it.

Of course, this also had its disadvantages. I had to do absolutely everything myself — from writing code and configuring servers to creating advertising banners and designing projects. However, this allowed me to gradually develop not only my programming skills but also my design abilities.

Some time later, something happened that I did not expect. One of the bots I was developing suddenly reached its peak in popularity. More than 100,000 users have used it over time, and the income turned out to be much higher than anything I had previously dreamed of.

Thanks to this, I was able to buy a good computer and everything else I needed for comfortable work. But for me, it was important not only because of the equipment — it became proof that what I was doing could actually work.

There have been many different moments along my journey. There were projects I was proud of, as well as situations when it seemed that everything was coming to an end. There were mistakes, failures, and decisions that I had to make almost blindly.

But I never seriously considered giving up. I continued trying, redesigning, and starting over.

Perhaps that is exactly what helped me make it this far.

This was my story — a journey from my first experiments on an old laptop to independently developing my own projects. On this website, you will find more of my work, notes, and materials about the process of creating projects. You will also be able to follow my progress and discover my new ideas.
